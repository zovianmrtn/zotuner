Microphone privacy

ZoTuner uses your microphone after you choose Start tuning. It measures pitch on your device. Your audio is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Select Listening to stop the microphone. You can also revoke microphone access in your browser’s site settings.

Saved preferences

Your tuning preset, mode, concert pitch, reference volume and selected input are saved in this browser. Clear this site’s browser data to remove them.
